About

Marquee casts a cinematic now-playing card from your media server to a Google Nest Hub — five templates, live preview, weather on the Street scene, all self-hosted.

—
github.com/Jamisonfitz/marquee
Export on the Connection tab copies your whole setup (presets included)
Credits

Marquee stands on generous shoulders:

  • TRusselo — Emby & Jellyfin backends, session filters and rotation, the dead-card heartbeat, the content filter, and a steady stream of sharp fixes.
  • catt by Stavros Korokithakis — the casting engine that actually puts the card on your Hub (BSD licensed, bundled stock).
  • The weather effects adapt open CodePen techniques by sheepjs, Ivan Odintsov, Braeden Craig, and Tiff Wong — full notes in CREDITS.md.
